## How do I delete my data?

You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Visit the [Legal & Privacy contact form](/contact?subject=legal) and submit a deletion request. We'll process it within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is your GDPR compliance?

We're GDPR compliant. You have the right to access, rectify, export, and erase your personal data. We process GitHub profile and contribution data only to generate the evidence reports you request — we never sell your data.

## What personal data do you collect?

When you connect your GitHub account, we store your GitHub username, public repositories, and contribution metadata. If you're a candidate, we also store the email associated with your GitHub OAuth login. Full details are in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## What are your Terms of Service?

Our Terms of Service cover acceptable use of the platform, data ownership, and limitations of liability. You can read the full [Terms of Service](/terms) — we keep them in plain English.
